Category: Smart Infrastructure
01

Problem Statement

India's civil construction sector suffers from 12–18% cost leakage caused by opaque 3-tier broker markups, unfair daily wage deductions (20–30%), transit highway toll disputes, and unmonitored site material stock-outs that halt construction.

⚠️ Cost Loss: ₹12–18L per ₹1 Cr project Zero Visibility
02

Proposed Solution

BuildNexus is an end-to-end digital ConTech operating system connecting Builders, Suppliers, Trade Squads, and Homeowners. It provides transparent wholesale procurement, sq.ft-based labor contracts, real-time site stock tracking, and OTP-verified delivery.

Direct Procurement 100% Fair Pay Zero Stock-out
04

Competitor Analysis

Unlike lead directories that only sell unverified phone contacts, or basic accounting apps, BuildNexus owns the entire transactional lifecycle.

IndiaMART / Justdial Unverified leads only
Khatabook / Ledgers No logistics or labor
BuildNexus Complete Verified OS
03

Uniqueness & Innovation

Core Moat
🛡️ 100% Guaranteed Fair Wages

Direct worker payouts calculated per sq.ft area with zero platform deductions (0% cut on labor wages).

🔒 In-App Private Negotiation

Auto-masks personal phone numbers in chat to eliminate bypass, ensure safety, and protect bulk wholesale prices.

⏳ Site Stock Depletion Horizon

Monitors daily consumption and alerts builders with a countdown ("3 Days of Cement Left") for 1-click reordering.

🤖 NexusBot AI Construction Copilot

Estimates Bill of Quantities (BOQ) instantly from sq.ft area and forecasts 30-day commodity price trends (Cement & Steel).

Social Impact + Enterprise ConTech
